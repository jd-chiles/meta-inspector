# Decisions and changes

## Current decisions — October 26, 2026

1. **Primary direction:** Habitability Walkthrough Companion for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, Indie Developer / Build for Your World.
2. **Impact boundary:** Improve one administrative walkthrough step; do not claim to reduce time to permanent housing.
3. **Prototype:** Image-first, three or four verified form items, review-only evidence cards, worker control.
4. **AI boundary:** Muse Spark drafts neutral observations with uncertainty. It does not decide pass/fail, certify habitability, or sign the form.
5. **Stretch:** Add Voice Transcribe, then optional SAM highlighting, only after the core path is reliable.
6. **Data boundary:** Synthetic or self-shot mock unit only; no tenant data or identifying footage.
7. **Research boundary:** Case-manager input is qualitative. Quantified claims from early notes are not cleared without authoritative verification.

## Superseded early framing

The initial sketch described all 11 Form 1083 items, automated deficiency flags, on-device/API vision, HMIS-ready packet generation, landlord fix lists with deadlines, offline sync, and an impact claim about speeding permanent placements. A later concept draft also brainstormed broad multimodal integrations and ambitious packet workflows.

These are not the current build commitment. The feasibility review found the active form version unresolved, the field counts and tests unverified, provider acceptance unknown, and placement-time impact unsupported. Keep these ideas as future possibilities only after provider validation.

An early evidence block also contained specific claims about 30-day subsidy holds, roughly 50% reinspections, “days off every placement,” and process-wide bottlenecks. Those claims require direct authoritative sources before reuse; they are intentionally not part of the current pitch.
