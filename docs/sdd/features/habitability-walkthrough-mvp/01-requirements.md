# Requirements — Move-in Ready video walkthrough (Brief v1)

Source of product scope: owner-provided Product Brief v1 and concept rendering, received 2026-10-29. These requirements supersede the earlier photo-first REQ-002 and the October 29 statement that video was out of scope. LAHSA Form 1083 identity/use remains owner-confirmed; this app's file acceptance and HMIS connection are not.

## REQ-001 — Form and funding intake

Model the 11 LAHSA Form 1083 areas in form order using stable IDs and the owner-provided labels. Fire Safety includes separate 11a unit-detector and 11b public/common-area detector checks. Ask for funding type before capture. HUD-funded selection routes to a clear HQS-required state and blocks this LAHSA branch. Use only a pseudonym and staged/mock-unit data. The demo leaves participant/HMIS identity and unit address blank, so its Form 1083 export is not case-ready. Do not claim the app submits to HMIS.

## REQ-002 — Room-based video capture

Provide capture/upload for kitchen, bathroom, bedroom/living, and hallway clips. Show short, room-specific prompts (including faucet on, fan running, door lock/unlock, and detector visibility). Preview, replace, remove, and recover a clip. Keep bounded MP4/MOV H.264/H.265/MPEG-4 video locally for review. Before live analysis, normalize a supported MOV to MP4 with a bounded local packet-copy remux; do not re-encode. Validate the normalized bytes and required tracks. A failed conversion preserves the prior clip and blocks upload. Send only MP4 to hosted SAM because Meta's current Files API documents video/mp4 as its video MIME. Validate actual bytes, size, duration, and decoded stills; clip and room identity are bound to a fresh ID/hash/version. Do not send client URLs or persist evidence in service-worker caches.

## REQ-003 — Server-only Meta SAM and Muse pipeline

Use the allowlisted Meta Model API with the Standard/non-training sam-3.1 model for per-room, one-concept-per-request video segmentation and muse-spark-1.3 for mapping segmented moments to form elements. The browser sends the selected MP4 once in stage=segment; after SAM returns, the server verifies the submitted clip hash and uses server-side FFmpeg to decode actual candidate frames plus one source context still near the center of each second (at most 30). Cache only bounded JPEGs in the expiring one-time in-memory mapping lease, keyed to the full video identity and signed candidate token. The later stage=map request carries identity and token only; it sends no clip or image bytes. Muse receives only the server-derived candidate/context stills for the validated clip. If Meta Muse is unavailable for a retryable provider failure and the owner has configured BASETEN_API_KEY, retry mapping once through the fixed Baseten Muse Spark Open endpoint; this fallback never replaces SAM and never runs for validation, setup, schema, or safety failures. Disclose that fallback and its data transfer in the UI. Keep credentials, file IDs, upload responses, raw provider bodies, and media out of browser bundles and logs. Provider calls require explicit live mode and a real key; errors fail closed with safe codes and no fixture fallback. Upload files with the shortest supported expiry and disclose temporary provider processing in the UI.

## REQ-004 — Unreviewed evidence-linked draft suggestions

Every model suggestion remains visibly an AI draft. A proposed A or D requires the exact bounded server-derived JPEG bytes that Muse received, with a matching SHA-256 and candidate frame identity from the hash-verified current submitted video; otherwise return needs human check and do not attach that still. Persist and display those same verified JPEG bytes for the cited evidence. Preserve model confidence as a draft-only signal, never as confidence in a final inspection result. Validate IDs against the fixed 11-element catalog, timestamps/frame IDs against submitted media, bounds, and strict output shapes. Reject extra or final-verdict fields. Model correction text and dates remain proposals until a worker edits/confirms them.

## REQ-005 — Independent evaluator decisions and sign-off

Each area begins without an evaluator decision. The evaluator records A, D, or needs-human-check separately from the model proposal, and can correct a proposal while preserving both values and timestamps. Fire Safety 11a (unit detectors) and 11b (public/common-area detectors) are independent; the aggregate Fire Safety result is D if either is D, A only if both are A, and otherwise unresolved. Every saved A or D requires a structured in-person-check acknowledgment with the same evaluator identity and a completion time no later than the decision time; unreviewed and needs-human-check states do not require one. Prior saved A/D decisions without a valid acknowledgment return to unreviewed during migration, while their clips and proposals remain intact. An evidence-less D is not allowed. A worker-confirmed D requires a correction, completion date, and reinspection date. The final result is derived only from evaluator decisions; no AI action can approve, deny, sign, or clear a required human check. Signing requires saved videos for all four REQ-002 rooms, all 11 areas resolved, all required fields complete, evaluator name, and explicit attestation that the evaluator assessed the unit to the best of their ability and is not a HUD-certified inspector. Include optional agency-leadership sign-off when required.

## REQ-006 — Evidence identity and async safety

Bind each clip, frame, segmentation response, model suggestion, and evaluator decision to session ID, room/element, schema version and catalog version, media ID/hash, analysis attempt, and revision. Late responses after replacement, room change, cancellation, or deletion cannot overwrite current evidence or restore a removed session. Reanalysis invalidates prior suggestions/decisions for affected evidence and requires human review again. No duplicate billed retry; retry is user-initiated.

## REQ-007 — Local persistence, recovery, and deletion

Persist pseudonym, funding type, room clips, extracted stills, suggestions, evaluator decisions, signature, and exports' source state in versioned IndexedDB. Recover after refresh and offline periods. Handle quota/private-mode errors with a clear in-memory-only warning. Clear-session removes local clips, stills, model drafts, decisions, signatures, and queued work. Explain that local deletion does not erase provider-side processing or downloaded files. The service worker caches only app shell/assets; no media, keys, or provider payloads.

## REQ-008 — Three exports

Generate separate local PDFs for client file, landlord fix list (only evaluator-confirmed D items, each with actual still, correction, completion and reinspection dates), and the official LAHSA FY 2022–23 Form 1083 plus structured JSON. The HMIS-ready PDF uses the unchanged official form as its base, fills supported fields, flattens it, and appends local evidence/continuation pages. It separately marks 11a and 11b from their corresponding evaluator assessments. Planned reinspection dates appear in notes; the official “Date Reinspection Completed” field stays blank until an actual completed reinspection is recorded. Mock-only participant/HMIS identity and unit-address fields remain blank, so this file is not case-ready. PDFs include signature/attestation and evidence provenance, escape worker/model text, and carry an explicit local/manual-upload notice. Pending, rejected, unsupported, or needs-human-check model text is not presented as an approved finding. Do not claim direct submission or provider acceptance. An unsigned/unresolved inspection cannot export as a completed signed record.

## REQ-009 — Mobile-first three-step interface

Match the supplied mobile reference's visual hierarchy and interaction pattern: Video Capture, Draft Review, Sign-Off & Export. Keep Capture as the first/default screen and present one room/element task at a time. On phones, keep the selected room and saved-clip count in a compact header; put other room choices behind a closed-by-default “Change room” control. Keep the camera preview and record/upload actions ahead of the prompt card. Show the active filming prompt plus two nearby steps as compact scannable rows, highlight the current prompt, and provide an explicit way to view the full room checklist. A filming prompt is complete only when its associated room clip is successfully saved locally after recording, or when an existing saved clip for that same room is opened in review. An unsaved recording and a clip from another room or session never complete the prompt. On phone widths, use a safe-area-aware three-item bottom navigation for Review/Sign-Off/Profile; Capture remains the initial screen and contextual back paths return to it. Keep the next-step action clear without covering content. Each primary step is a distinct screen; do not stack Capture, Review, and Sign-Off as one continuous mobile page. At 360×640 and 390×844 CSS-pixel viewports, measure the vertical gap from the last visible Capture prompt/action to the top edge of the safe-area bottom navigation: it must be no more than 32 CSS px when the content fits; when content exceeds the available pane, only the main pane scrolls and no actionable control is obscured. At both viewports, document scroll width must not exceed viewport width. Through 959px, keep review overview and one-element assessment in separate focused states. At desktop widths from 960px, use a centered workspace, sticky three-item navigation that does not cover content, a split review list/one-element assessment, and a two-column capture view. Keep provider mode details with the consent and analysis controls instead of persistent mobile chrome. Use visible status text, accessible labels/focus, keyboard actions, announced errors, and upload fallback when camera capture is unavailable.

## REQ-010 — Bounded local video API

Accept only same-origin multipart requests with server-owned prompt catalogs, fixed room/element enums, allowlisted provider host, bounded duration/bytes/frame count, one active operation per session, timeouts, and abort propagation. For stage=segment, validate actual input bytes and derive candidate/context JPEGs from that same clip using FFmpeg; cache bounded bytes in a one-time process-local lease. Require an FFmpeg executable with supported video decoders and MJPEG encoding, using ffmpeg from PATH by default or server-only FFMPEG_PATH. For stage=map, accept identity and signed token only and reject client-supplied clip or still bytes; do not upload the clip a second time. Never fetch user URLs. Return sanitized frame indices/timestamps, confidence, and safe error codes only. No automatic billed retries. No public unauthenticated deployment.

## REQ-011 — Evaluation and demo honesty

Use mock-unit media and a pseudonym only. Keep fixture examples explicitly synthetic and separate from live model results. The 30-second kitchen/bathroom test clip is the release gate for segmentation tracking, element mapping, timestamp/still completeness, and indoor-light miss review. No generated test outcome, fabricated metrics, or demo script before the miss list. Fixture tests do not prove provider behavior or the clip gate.

## REQ-012 — CI and independent review

Keep the SDD request, requirements, design, plan, verification, and task mappings current. Run the configured docs, lint, typecheck, unit, browser, and build checks. Do not weaken assertions, skip the new flow, expose secrets, or claim native SDD approval/review/evidence that was not recorded. The project owner designates the implementation agent; that agent reports executed checks and independent findings accurately.

## REQ-013 — Offline-first app shell

Retain the installable PWA shell and local session recovery. Offline capture/review can continue locally; live segmentation is clearly pending and runs only after an explicit connected retry. The service worker never caches clips, frames, provider bodies, or keys.

## REQ-014 — Step state and review overview

Capture records room clips and prompt completion; collapsed clip details remain reachable above mobile navigation. Draft Review opens with a four-row compact overview and a separate “View all 11 elements” screen, then shows one selected element's proposal mark, draft confidence, actual still/time, worker decision and correction status. Returning from All Elements through the header restores the compact summary and clears the focused item. “Add note” opens a focused editor and saves a bounded note to this device's walkthrough record; it never changes a decision, enters an export, or triggers a provider request. Sign-Off & Export shows the evaluator result, signature/attestation, and the three export actions. These primary steps render one at a time and share the three-item phone navigation; they are not one continuous page. Progress labels never imply model approval. Profile contains pseudonym/funding settings, Form 1083 administrative fields, offline status, privacy/cost notes, and local reset. Required setup fields are presented one panel at a time in Unit & Funding → Evaluator → Landlord & Agency, with per-panel completion state. The landlord-discussion date appears only after YES is selected and is required when shown.

## REQ-015 — Funding route and sign-off fields

Non-HUD LAHSA units may continue into Form 1083. HUD-funded units receive a prominent HQS branch message and cannot generate a Form 1083 completion result from this app. Record evaluator name/typed signature/signed time, agency name, landlord discussion YES/NO and date when YES, and optional leadership name/title/signature/date when required. The mock-only participant/HMIS identity and unit address remain blank. No voucher, funding release, landlord delivery, direct HMIS submission, or reinspection scheduling action is implemented.
