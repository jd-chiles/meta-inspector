# Initial concept sketch (superseded)

This was the starting brainstorm, not the current product commitment. See [Decisions](docs/DECISIONS.md) and the [product brief](docs/PRODUCT_BRIEF.md) for the validated, narrower direction.

> A mobile-first tool that walks a Housing Navigator through the Form 1083 inspection once, captures per-item photo evidence, flags likely deficiencies with vision models, and generates a reviewable packet.

The first sketch proposed all 11 items, photo capture, AI deficiency suggestions, one-click PDF/checklist/fix-list export, and seeded pass/fail/reinspection examples. It also hypothesized major system bottlenecks and quantified impact. Those details are not verified: the operative form version, fields, physical tests, provider workflow, impact statistics, and packet acceptance all need confirmation. Do not present the early figures or automated deficiency claims as established facts.
